# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report security problems in a public issue, discussion or pull request.

- **Preferred**: use **Report a vulnerability** under the affected repository's **Security** tab, which opens a private advisory only the maintainers can see.
- **Otherwise**: email [support@abnormalhq.com](mailto:support@abnormalhq.com) with a subject starting with `[security]`.

Include what is affected (repository, site or app and its version), how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could gain. Do not include real customer data; if you came across some, tell us what kind it was rather than sending it.

We aim to acknowledge a report within three working days and to keep you updated until it is fixed. Please give us reasonable time to fix the problem before disclosing it. We are glad to credit you in the fix unless you would rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope: the code in the abnormalhq repositories and the services they run, including abnormalhq.com, webmail.abnormalhq.com, console.abnormalhq.com, developers.abnormalhq.com, status.abnormalhq.com, and the desktop, mobile and command-line clients.

Out of scope: denial-of-service and load testing, spam or social engineering against our staff or users, physical attacks, and reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact. Test only against accounts and mailboxes you own, and stop and report as soon as you can reach another person's data.

## Supported versions

Fixes go to the latest release of each client and to what is deployed from `main`. Please check that a problem still exists there before reporting it.

# Getting help

- **Is the service down?** Check <https://status.abnormalhq.com> first; you can subscribe there to updates by email or Atom feed.
- **Setting up a mailbox**: the guide at <https://abnormalhq.com/guide/> covers webmail, mail apps, and Claude and ChatGPT.
- **Building on the API**: see <https://developers.abnormalhq.com>.
- **A mailbox, an order, a payment or a redeem code**: email [support@abnormalhq.com](mailto:support@abnormalhq.com) with your order number or mailbox address (never your password).
- **A bug or an idea for one of these repositories**: open an issue in that repository.
- **A security problem**: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening an issue.
